The Sapienza University of Rome (Wisdom) / Sapienza Università di Roma has existed since the beginning of the 14th century (year 1303). The number of students is about 120,000, and the staff is approximately 8,000. 15% of programs at the university are in English, and the rest are in the Italian language.

- Language preparatory course: 5,000 euros/year
- Bachelor's degree (3 years) 2.821-2.924 euros/year
- Master's degree (2 years) 2.821-2.924 euros/year

- Political Science Medicine

- Information technology Economics
- Humanities Architecture
- Start of studies: Autumn
- Dormitory: 400 euro/month
- Meals: from 165 euros/month
