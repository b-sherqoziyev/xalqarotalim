**Riga Technical University (RTU)** (Rīgas Tehniskā Universitāte (RTU)) is a prestigious oldest (since October 14, year 1862) modern university in the city of Riga (the capital of Latvia). An innovative research center of Latvia of international level with a Large scientific library, student dormitory, sports grounds, parks.

### FACULTIES

**Bachelor’s (3-4 years)** 3000-4700 euro/year:

- Economics
- Chemical Technology
- Organization and Management of International Economic Relations
- Electronics and Mobile Communication
- Aviation Transport
- Computer Systems
- Technical Translation
- RailwayEngineering
- Engineering Technology, Mechanics and Mechanical Engineering
- Telecommunications
- Entrepreneurship and Management

Medical Engineering and Physics

**Master’s(1-2 years)** 2900-5500 euro/year:

- Creative Industries and Growth Management
- Economics
- Entrepreneurship and Management
- Organization and Management of International Economic Relations
- Computer Systems
- Civil Construction and Real Estate Management
- Business Finance
- Technical Translation
- Logistics and Supply Chain Management
- Start of studies: Autumn
- Dormitory: 200-220 euros
- Meals: 170-250 euros
